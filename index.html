A bit of history

  • Napoleon was born in Ajaccio in 1769 — his childhood home, Maison Bonaparte, is now a museum, about an hour and a half's drive from the villas.~1h 30min · Map
  • Just before that, Corsica was briefly its own country: the Corsican Republic (1755–1769), led by Pasquale Paoli, had one of the world's first written constitutions — drafted in Tuscan Italian and unusually progressive for its time, including a form of female suffrage. France annexed the island in 1769, the same year Napoleon was born.
  • The Knights Templar passed through Corsica too — the Gothic Église Saint-Dominique in Bonifacio (the only Gothic-style church on the island) was built by Dominican friars on what's believed to be the site of an earlier Templar church, worth a look on the Bonifacio day trip.
  • Corsica changed hands a lot: Roman, Pisan, then centuries of Genoese rule (hence the watchtowers dotted along the coast) before France took over — you can see the layers of it in the old towns at Bonifacio and Porto-Vecchio.

Questions

FAQ

Are towels and bed linen provided?

Yes — bed linen plus shower, beach and pool towels are all provided in both villas.

Is there a cleaning service during the stay?

Yes — one full clean and change of sheets is offered mid-stay, on the middle weekend (around Sat 15/Sun 16 Aug), at a time the host decides. If the villa needs a proper clean again before departure, that can be arranged directly with the host for an extra fee.

What's already in the villa?

A Nespresso machine and a filter coffee machine, hairdryers in every bathroom, a safe in every bedroom, and three Sonos speakers in the living space.

Can breakfast be arranged?

Yes, through the host — €18 per person: tea, coffee or hot chocolate, pastries, bread with butter and jam, fromage blanc, and fresh orange juice.

Can groceries be delivered?

Yes — see Everyday Essentials for the SPAR and Super U ordering links.

What if I end up at the wrong place?

Corsica has a couple of similarly-named spots, so double-check you're on the south-east coast, right by the sea, before going too far. Search "Villa Aria Alto" or "Villa Aria Terra" directly in Maps, and call the host about 30 minutes out.
